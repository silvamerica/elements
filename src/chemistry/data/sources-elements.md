# Element and world data sources

`elements.json` and `worlds.json` are generated by
`node scripts/build-elements.mjs` (set `BUILD_ELEMENTS_CACHE=<dir>` to
cache downloads; Wikipedia rate-limits bursts). Every number comes from one
of the sources below, fetched at build time. Nothing was typed in from
memory except the small atmosphere and Jupiter tables, which were copied
from the pages they cite and are commented in the script.

## elements.json

Names, symbols and standard atomic masses (masses used only for the
mass-to-atom conversions) come from Bowserinator/Periodic-Table-JSON,
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Bowserinator/Periodic-Table-JSON/master/PeriodicTableJSON.json.

`color` is Jmol's CPK table, `argbsCpk` in
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BobHanson/Jmol-SwingJS/master/src/org/jmol/c/PAL.java.
All 109 entries match the "J" (Jmol) column of Wikipedia "CPK coloring". Jmol
defines no colour above Mt (109). Ds through Og (110–118) get Jmol's
unknown-element colour, `Xx` = `#FF1493`, so those nine share one hot pink.

`radiusPm` comes from Wikipedia "Atomic radii of the elements (data page)".
It uses the empirical column (Slater 1964), then Calculated (Clementi 1967),
then Covalent (single bond). Kr 88, Xe 108, At 127 and Rn 120 use the
calculated value. Where that page has nothing usable (Fr, Bk–No, and Lr–Og),
the value is the Pyykkö & Atsumi 2009 single-bond covalent radius from the
"Radius for multiple bonds" grid on Wikipedia "Covalent radius". The data
page's empirical values for He (120) and Ne (160) are marked "citation
needed" and are not Slater's. Ar is 71. They are kept as published but are
inconsistent with the rest of the column.

`natural` is true for Z 1–92 except Tc and Pm. `noble` covers He, Ne, Ar, Kr,
Xe, Rn and Og.

`cosmic` is the Lodders (2003) ApJ 591:1220 Table 6 solar-system abundance in
atoms per 10^6 Si, summed over isotopes. It comes from MESA's
machine-readable copy,
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MESAHub/mesa/main/chem/data/lodders03.data,
which gives H = 2.431e10 and Si = 1.000e6. The value is null for every
element that table omits: Tc, Pm, Po, At, Rn, Fr, Ra, Ac, Pa, and Np onward.

## worlds.json

All values are atom (mole) fractions. Mass data was divided by atomic mass,
and each world was normalised to sum to 1. Only `natural` elements are kept.
Elements a source lists with no value were left out.

ocean uses the CRC Handbook seawater column W1 (kg/L) from Wikipedia
"Abundances of the elements (data page)", Sea water section. It covers 81
elements and includes the H and O of water.

crust uses the CRC Handbook continental crust column C1 (kg/kg) from the
same data page. It covers 88 elements.

atmosphere starts from dry-air mole fractions in ppm taken from Wikipedia
"Atmosphere of Earth" (Allen's Astrophysical Quantities 2002): N2 780840,
O2 209460, Ar 9340, Ne 18.2, He 5.24 and Kr 1.14. CO2 is 423.9 and CH4 is
1.94, the 2024 values from the WMO Greenhouse Gas Bulletin No. 21 cited on
that page. Three gases are not in that table. H2 is 0.55 ppm from the NASA
Earth Fact Sheet (https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html).
Xe is 87 nL/L from Wikipedia "Xenon". N2O is 339 ppb (2025) from Wikipedia
"Nitrous oxide". The dry gases were normalised to 1. Water vapour is 0.25%
of the atmosphere by mass ("Atmosphere of Earth"), which works out to
0.40 mol H2O per 100 mol dry air using dry-air molar mass 28.964 and
H2O 18.015. Molecules were then counted as atoms, so N2 gives 2 N and CO2
gives 1 C and 2 O. Other reactive trace species (O3, CO, SO2 and so on) are
not included.

cell uses the "Mass (kg)" column for a 70 kg adult (Emsley 2011) from
Wikipedia "Composition of the human body", elemental composition list. It
covers 65 elements. Ge has no kg value. It uses the table's mass-fraction
sort key, 5e-8, times 70 kg. That cell's displayed {{val}} is 5e-6, but the
5e-8 sort key fits Ge's position in the kg-sorted table, while 5e-6 would
put it above Cu.

gasgiant uses element/H ratios equal to solar X/H times Jupiter/Sun
(Atreya et al. 2003), from the table in Wikipedia "Atmosphere of Jupiter",
for He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, C, N, O, P and S. N uses the 8 bar enrichment of 3.6.
O uses the midpoint (0.385) of the Galileo probe's deepest 19 bar range,
0.19–0.58. The page notes the true deep O is probably about 3x solar, so
water is likely under-represented. Ge and As (germane, arsine) are described
only as "trace", with no numbers, so they are omitted.

meteorite uses the Palme & O'Neill (2014) CI chondrite composition,
Treatise on Geochemistry 2nd ed. 3:1–39, via pyrolite,
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/morganjwilliams/pyrolite/main/pyrolite/data/geochem/refcomp/CH_PalmeONeill2014.csv.
It covers 78 elements and is given as % or ppm by mass. The requested
Lodders (2003) CI table (Table 3) could not be fetched in machine-readable
form: VizieR/CDS timed out, and IOP and ADS returned 403. It is not on
Wikipedia. Palme & O'Neill is a later CI compilation by Lodders' peers. It
has no noble gases.
